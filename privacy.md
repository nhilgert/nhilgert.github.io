# Privacy policy

This OAuth client is used only by its owner, Nick Hilgert, to sync his own Google Drive with
[rclone](https://rclone.org).

- It accesses only the owner's Google Drive, and only to copy files the owner chooses.
- It does not collect, sell, or share any data with anyone.
- Access tokens are stored only in the owner's own accounts.
- The owner can revoke access at any time at
  [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions).
